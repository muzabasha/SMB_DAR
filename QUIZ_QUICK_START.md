# Quiz System - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 What's New?

Interactive feedback-based quizzes have been added to each unit! Test your knowledge with immediate feedback and detailed explanations.

## 📍 Where to Find Quizzes

1. Open the application: http://localhost:61931
2. Go to **Dashboard**
3. Scroll down to **"📝 Unit Quizzes"** section
4. Click on any unit quiz button

## 🎮 How to Take a Quiz

### Step 1: Start Quiz
- Click on a unit quiz button (Unit 1, 2, 3, or 4)
- Quiz loads with first question

### Step 2: Answer Question
- Read the question carefully
- Select one of the 4 options (A, B, C, or D)
- Click on your answer

### Step 3: Get Feedback
- **If Correct:** ✅ Green highlight + explanation
- **If Incorrect:** ❌ Red highlight + correct answer + explanation
- Read the explanation to learn why

### Step 4: Continue
- Click "Next Question" button
- Repeat for all 5 questions

### Step 5: View Results
- After last question, see your score
- View performance breakdown
- Get recommendations if needed

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I retake a quiz?**
A: Yes! Click "Retake Quiz" after finishing. Questions reset and you can try again.

**Q: Are my scores saved?**
A: Currently, scores are only saved during your session. Refresh the page and they reset.

**Q: Can I skip questions?**
A: No, you must answer each question to proceed.

**Q: How many questions per quiz?**
A: 5 questions per unit (20 total across all units).

**Q: What if I get a question wrong?**
A: You'll see the correct answer and a detailed explanation. Click "Next Question" to continue.

**Q: Can I go back to previous questions?**
A: No, quizzes are linear. You move forward through questions.

**Q: How long does a quiz take?**
A: About 5-10 minutes per unit quiz.

**Q: Are quizzes graded?**
A: No, these are practice quizzes for self-assessment. Scores are not submitted anywhere.
